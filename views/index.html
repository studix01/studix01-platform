About Studix

Studix is an online learning platform dedicated to helping students across Rwanda and beyond achieve academic success. We provide free access to quality learning resources, including study materials, practice exercises, interactive quizzes, and AI-powered learning assistance.

Our mission is to make quality education accessible to every student, regardless of their background or location. We believe that with the right tools and support, every student can excel.
